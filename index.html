About Us

Streetlink Technologies is a technology company founded in 2020 and headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya. Built on years of hands-on experience dating back to 2020, we specialize in fast internet installation, CCTV surveillance, solar energy solutions, website development, laptop sales and technical support.

Our internet services currently cover Nairobi, Machakos, Machakos Junction, Matiliku and Makindu,with offices in each town to serve you better. For CCTV, solar, web development and technical support, distance is no barrier,we are ready to serve you wherever you are across Kenya.

Our mission is simple: to make quality technology accessible, affordable and dependable for every home and business we serve.
